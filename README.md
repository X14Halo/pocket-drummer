# Pocket Drummer

Realistic drum grooves to jam along with on guitar. Pick a style, tap a tempo, slide intensity, busyness and swing, or hit **Surprise me** for a fresh groove. 259 grooves across rock, blues, shuffle, country, jazz, funk, soul, pop, reggae, latin and heavy, in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 and 12/8. Turn on **Fills** for up to four drum fills that take turns at the end of each phrase. Every fill is a real performance by a session drummer, matched to the groove's style, feel and tempo, and each slot has its own length, complexity, intensity and build-up; with fills on, downloads contain one full run of them. Save presets (and export or import them as files to back up, share or move to another device) and download loops as WAV, MP3 or MIDI (.mid or .smf).

**Play it:** https://x14halo.github.io/pocket-drummer/

## Sounds

- Drum kit: [Virtuosity Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums) by Versilian Studios (CC0), with the kick and toms tuned slightly lower and lightly dampened
- Tambourine and shaker: VSCO 2 Community Edition, via Virtuosity Drums (CC0)

The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html`.
