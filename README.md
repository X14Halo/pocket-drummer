# Pocket Drummer

Realistic drum grooves to jam along with on guitar. Pick a style, tap a tempo, slide intensity, busyness and swing, or hit **Surprise me** for a fresh groove. 297 grooves, including 38 "Session" grooves played by real drummers, across rock, blues, shuffle, country, jazz, funk, soul, pop, reggae, latin and heavy, in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8 and 12/8. Turn on **Fills** for up to four drum fills that take turns at the end of each phrase. Every fill is a real performance by a session drummer, matched to the groove's style, feel and tempo, and each slot has its own length, complexity, intensity and build-up; with fills on, downloads contain one full run of them. Save presets (and export or import them as files to back up, share or move to another device) and download loops as WAV, MP3 or MIDI (.mid or .smf).

**Play it:** https://x14halo.github.io/pocket-drummer/

## Sounds

- Drum kits: three custom kits rendered from free, license-free samples (close mics, plus overhead and room mics): Standard (a modern studio kit), 70s (dry and deadened) and Jazz. Auto keeps the standard kit for most styles and uses the 70s kit for soul, funk and reggae and the jazz kit for jazz and bossa nova; the Drum kit switcher overrides it. The kits play back exactly as rendered with no added compression or EQ, and the Room & reverb menu only adds reverb from the overhead and room mics. The 70s and jazz kits load from `kits/` the first time they're needed
- Tambourine and shaker: VSCO 2 Community Edition, via Virtuosity Drums (CC0)

The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html`.
