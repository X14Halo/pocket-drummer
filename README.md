# Pocket Drummer

Realistic drum grooves to jam along with on guitar. Pick a style, tap a tempo, slide intensity, busyness and swing, or hit **Surprise me** for a fresh groove. 146 grooves across rock, blues, shuffle, country, jazz, funk, soul, pop, reggae, latin and heavy, in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 12/8 and 5/4. Save presets and export loops as MP3, WAV and MIDI.

**Play it:** https://x14halo.github.io/pocket-drummer/

## Sounds

- Drum kit: [Big Rusty Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums) by Karoryfer Samples (CC0)
- Tambourine and shaker: VSCO 2 Community Edition, via [Virtuosity Drums](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums) (CC0)

The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html`.
